# Contract Review Tab — What It Actually Does

If the **Cases Tab** is the command center for disputes,

then the **Contract Review Tab** is the command center for contracts.

Its purpose is not just to store contracts.

Its purpose is to **understand contracts**.

---

# Core Mission

A lawyer uploads a contract and immediately gets answers to questions like:

* What are the risks?
* What obligations exist?
* What clauses are missing?
* Is the arbitration clause enforceable?
* What liabilities am I accepting?
* What changed between versions?
* What should I negotiate?

Think:

> Contract Review = AI-powered legal due diligence.

---

# What Happens When a Contract Is Uploaded?

User uploads:

```text
Service_Agreement.pdf
```

The system automatically:

### Step 1: Extract Text

* OCR if scanned
* Parse PDF/DOCX
* Identify structure

---

### Step 2: Detect Clauses

AI identifies:

```text
Definitions
Term
Termination
Confidentiality
Indemnity
Liability
Force Majeure
Dispute Resolution
Governing Law
Payment Terms
```

---

### Step 3: Analyze

AI reviews:

* obligations
* rights
* risks
* unusual provisions
* missing protections

---

### Step 4: Generate Findings

Example:

```text
High Risk:
Unlimited liability clause

Medium Risk:
No force majeure clause

Low Risk:
Standard confidentiality provision
```

# The Ultimate Goal

When a lawyer uploads a 100-page contract, they should not need to read every page first.

Within minutes, CIMA AI should tell them:

* what the contract does
* who owes what
* where the risks are
* what clauses are missing
* whether the dispute clause works
* what changed between drafts
* what should be renegotiated

In other words:

> The Contract Review Tab transforms contracts from static documents into structured, searchable, risk-scored legal intelligence.
