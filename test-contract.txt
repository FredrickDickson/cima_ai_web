SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement is entered into on January 1, 2025 between:

ABC Corporation ("Provider")
and
XYZ Company ("Client")

1. SERVICES
The Provider shall provide consulting services to the Client, including but not limited to strategic planning, market analysis, and technical advisory services.

2. TERM
This agreement shall commence on January 1, 2025 and continue for a period of 24 months, unless terminated earlier as provided herein.

3. PAYMENT
The Client shall pay the Provider $10,000 per month for the services rendered. Payment shall be made within 30 days of invoice.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY
Both parties agree to keep all information confidential and not to disclose any proprietary information to third parties without prior written consent.

5. LIABILITY
The Provider shall have unlimited liability for any breaches of this agreement, including but not limited to negligence, willful misconduct, and failure to perform services.

6. GOVERNING LAW
This agreement shall be governed by the laws of Ghana.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any disputes arising from this agreement shall be resolved through arbitration in Accra, Ghana.

8. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days written notice.

9. FORCE MAJEURE
This agreement is not currently subject to force majeure provisions.

10. INDEMNIFICATION
The Provider shall indemnify the Client against all claims, damages, and losses arising from the Provider's performance of services.
