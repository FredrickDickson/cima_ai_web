## Core Features Explained

### 1. Legal Research

**What it does:** Searches across thousands of legal authorities including case law, statutes, arbitration rules, and international treaties to find relevant precedents and legal principles.

**How to use it:**

1. **Ask Your Question in Plain Language**
   - Type your legal question naturally: "What are the grounds for challenging an arbitrator under ICC Rules?"
   - Use the microphone icon to dictate your question instead of typing
   - Click "Sharpen Query" to let AI refine your question for better results

2. **Filter Your Results**
   - Choose your jurisdiction (Ghana, International, England & Wales, etc.)
   - Select source types (Statutes, Case Law, Rules, Treaties)
   - Results show relevance scores so you know which sources matter most

3. **Review Your Results**
   - Each source shows its citation, jurisdiction, and an excerpt
   - Click "Read More" to see the full text
   - AI provides a synthesis that explains how the sources relate to your question

4. **Validate Authority**
   - Use the "Check Authority Status" button to verify if a case is still good law
   - See if cases have been followed, distinguished, or overruled
   - Get confidence scores for reliability

5. **Save and Export**
   - Copy results as rich text for your documents
   - All research is automatically saved to your history
   - Link research to specific cases for easy reference later

**Best for:** Finding legal precedents, understanding new legal concepts, preparing legal arguments, checking if cases are still valid.

### 2. Cases & Matters Management

**What it does:** Organizes all your legal matters—whether arbitration, litigation, or mediation—in one structured workspace where you can track deadlines, parties, documents, and progress.

**How to use it:**

1. **Create a New Case**
   - Click "New Case" and enter basic details:
     - Case title (e.g., "ABC Corp v. XYZ Ltd - Construction Dispute")
     - Matter number (your internal reference)
     - Type (Arbitration, Litigation, or Mediation)
     - Framework (ICC, UNCITRAL, LCIA, or Court-based)
   - Add parties with their roles (Claimant, Respondent, Arbitrator, etc.)
   - Write a brief description of the dispute

2. **Work Within Your Case**
   Once created, your case workspace has organized sections:
   
   - **Overview**: See all case details at a glance
   - **Issues**: Track the legal and factual issues in dispute
   - **Deadlines**: Add submission deadlines, hearing dates, and other time-sensitive tasks
   - **Hearings**: Schedule preliminary meetings, substantive hearings, and award dates
   - **Evidence**: Organize documents, witness statements, and exhibits
   - **Documents**: Link relevant contracts, briefs, and correspondence

3. **Track Urgency**
   - Color-coded status badges (Active, Pending, Closed, Settled)
   - Countdown timers for next hearings
   - Overdue deadline alerts in red

4. **Search and Filter**
   - Use the search bar to find cases by name or matter number
   - Filter by status to focus on active matters only
   - Sort by recent activity or upcoming deadlines

**Best for:** Managing multiple matters, tracking deadlines, organizing case documents, preparing for hearings, maintaining a complete case file.

### 3. Contract Review

**What it does:** Automatically analyzes contracts to identify risks, problematic clauses, missing provisions, and obligations—then suggests improvements.

**How to use it:**

1. **Upload Your Contract**
   - Drag and drop a PDF or Word document, or paste text directly
   - System accepts contracts in various formats

2. **Configure the Review**
   - Select document type (Commercial Contract, Employment Agreement, Lease, Arbitration Agreement, etc.)
   - Choose jurisdiction for compliance checking
   - Optionally link to an active case
   - Attach a "playbook" (your client's standards or preferences)

3. **Wait for Analysis** (usually 30-60 seconds)
   The system automatically:
   - Extracts and reads all clauses
   - Scores overall risk (0-100 scale)
   - Identifies problematic provisions
   - Checks for missing standard clauses
   - Extracts obligations for each party

4. **Review the Results**
   Navigate through organized tabs:

   **Overview Tab**
   - Risk gauge with color coding (green = low risk, red = high risk)
   - AI summary of the contract's purpose and key terms
   - Detected parties and governing law

   **Clauses Tab**
   - Every clause listed with risk assessment
   - Risk badges: Critical (red), High (orange), Medium (yellow), Low (green)
   - Click any clause to see:
     - What makes it risky
     - Suggested improvements
     - Market-standard alternatives
   - AI actions on each clause: Explain, Simplify, Rewrite, Make Mutual, Reduce Risk

   **Obligations Tab**
   - Side-by-side comparison of what each party must do
   - Clear breakdown of responsibilities

   **Missing Clauses Tab**
   - Standard clauses not found in the contract
   - Why each clause is important
   - Option to generate suggested language

   **Redlines Tab**
   - Visual markup showing proposed changes
   - Compare original vs. suggested version
   - Export marked-up contract

   **Arbitration Tab** (if applicable)
   - Specific analysis of arbitration clauses
   - Checks for enforceability
   - Identifies seat, institution, and governing rules
   - Suggests improvements

   **AI Insights Tab**
   - Strategic takeaways
   - Negotiation points
   - Commercial considerations

   **Q&A Tab**
   - Ask follow-up questions about the contract
   - Get instant AI answers with citations

5. **Take Action**
   - Export full analysis to Word or PDF
   - Save analysis for future reference
   - Use insights to negotiate better terms

**Best for:** Due diligence, contract negotiations, risk assessment, client advisory, identifying unfavorable terms before signing.

### 4. Drafting Studio

**What it does:** Helps you create legal documents from scratch or templates, with AI assistance to strengthen arguments, add citations, and ensure professional quality.

**How to use it:**

**Option A: Natural Language Drafting**
1. Describe what you want to draft in plain language:
   - "Draft a Notice of Arbitration for a $5 million construction dispute under ICC Rules"
   - "Create a non-disclosure agreement for a joint venture in Ghana"
2. Select jurisdiction and link to a case if relevant
3. Click "Generate Draft"
4. AI produces a complete first draft

**Option B: Template-Based Drafting**
1. Browse templates by category:
   - Arbitration (Notices, Statements of Claim, Awards)
   - Contracts (NDAs, Service Agreements, Joint Ventures)
   - Litigation (Pleadings, Motions, Briefs)
   - Corporate (Resolutions, Agreements)
2. Select your template
3. Fill in the blanks (party names, dates, amounts, etc.)
4. Add any special instructions
5. Generate document

**Editing and Enhancement**

Once your draft is generated, use the powerful editing tools:

**Left Panel - Your Document**
- Rich text editor with formatting options
- Three view modes:
  - **Full**: Complete formal document
  - **Short Form**: Condensed version
  - **Plain English**: Simple summary for non-lawyers
- Word count tracker
- Version history (undo/redo changes)

**Right Panel - AI Tools**

*AI Assistant Section*
- Ask for custom changes: "Make this section more persuasive"
- Get suggestions for improvement
- Request additional clauses or sections

*AI Actions on Selected Text*
Select any paragraph and choose:
- **Rewrite**: Make clearer and more professional
- **Strengthen Argument**: Add more persuasive language
- **Add Citations**: Insert relevant legal authorities
- **Simplify**: Make more accessible
- **Make Neutral**: Use balanced tone (for arbitrators)
- **Formalize**: Add legal language

*Document Review*
- Quick risk check on your draft
- Identifies ambiguities or unclear provisions
- Suggests missing sections

*Research Panel*
- Search for legal authorities without leaving the page
- Insert citations directly into your draft

*Clause Library*
- Browse standard clauses by category
- Insert with one click
- See variations and alternatives

**Keyboard Shortcuts**
- Press Ctrl+K (or Cmd+K) for Command Bar—type what you want AI to do

**Saving and Exporting**
- Click "Save Draft" to store in your workspace
- Export to Word or PDF for final delivery
- All versions are automatically saved

**Best for:** Creating arbitration documents, drafting contracts, preparing legal opinions, writing demand letters, generating awards and orders.

## Understanding Key Concepts

### Risk Scores
When analyzing contracts, CIMA AI assigns a risk score from 0-100:
- **0-30 (Green)**: Low risk - well-balanced contract
- **31-60 (Yellow)**: Medium risk - some concerns to address
- **61-85 (Orange)**: High risk - significant issues present
- **86-100 (Red)**: Critical risk - major problems requiring immediate attention

### Citations and Sources
Whenever AI provides information, it includes citations to legal authorities. Click these to verify the information and read the source yourself. This ensures you can trust the guidance and use it professionally.

### Authority Validation
The "Check Authority Status" feature tells you if a case is still "good law":
- **Followed**: Other courts have applied the same reasoning (strong authority)
- **Distinguished**: Courts have limited its application (use carefully)
- **Disapproved**: Courts have disagreed with it (weak authority)
- **Overruled**: No longer valid (do not use)

### Jurisdictions
CIMA AI specializes in:
- **African Jurisdictions**: Ghana, Kenya, Nigeria, South Africa
- **International Arbitration**: ICC, UNCITRAL, LCIA, AFSA
- **Other Systems**: England & Wales, New York Convention countries

Always select the correct jurisdiction for accurate results.

## Common Workflows

### Workflow 1: Reviewing a New Client Contract
1. Go to **Contract Review**
2. Upload the contract
3. Select document type and jurisdiction
4. Review the risk score and AI summary
5. Check the **Clauses** tab for problems
6. Review **Missing Clauses** section
7. Export the analysis with redlines
8. Share findings with your client

### Workflow 2: Preparing an Arbitration Notice
1. Go to **Cases** and create a new matter
2. Add parties, dispute details, and select ICC (or other framework)
3. Go to **Drafting Studio**
4. Select "Notice of Arbitration" template
5. Link to your new case
6. Fill in the variables (amounts, dates, claims)
7. Use AI actions to strengthen your statement of claim
8. Export to Word for filing

### Workflow 3: Researching a Legal Issue
1. Go to **Legal Research**
2. Type your question (e.g., "Can arbitrators award punitive damages?")
3. Select relevant jurisdictions (Ghana + International)
4. Review retrieved sources and AI analysis
5. Validate key cases with Citator
6. Copy findings for your memo
7. Link research to the relevant case

### Workflow 4: Preparing for a Hearing
1. Open your case in **Cases**
2. Check the **Hearings** tab for upcoming dates
3. Review linked **Evidence** and **Documents**
4. Go to **AI Assistant** (Context: Case Strategy)
5. Attach your case and key documents
6. Ask: "What are the strongest arguments for our position on [issue]?"
7. Use insights to prepare your oral submissions
8. Draft hearing notes in **Drafting Studio**

## Tips for Getting the Best Results

### Be Specific
Instead of "contract law in Ghana," ask "what are the requirements for a valid consideration under the Contracts Act 1960?"

### Use Context
When asking questions in AI Assistant, attach relevant documents or link to cases so AI has full context.

### Review AI Suggestions
AI is a powerful assistant, but always review its output with your professional judgment. Think of it as a first draft that you refine.

### Tag Authorities
Use @ mentions to reference specific cases or statutes. This helps AI provide more targeted analysis.

### Save Your Work
Use the save feature frequently in Drafting Studio and link documents to cases so nothing gets lost.

### Check Citations
Always verify that cited cases and statutes are correct by clicking through to read the source.

### Explore Templates
Don't start from scratch—browse templates in Drafting Studio to save time.
