# CIMA AI - Nordic Burgundy Color Palette

## Premium International Color Scheme

### Primary Colors
- **Nordic Burgundy**: `#5A2633` (Primary brand color - replaces `#8B0E1E`)
- **Warm Ivory**: `#F5F1E8` (Primary background - replaces `#FDFBF7` / `#F8F6F0`)
- **Charcoal**: `#252525` (Primary text - replaces `#2D2D2D`)
- **Muted Gold**: `#B49A67` (Accent - replaces `#C9A961`)

### Secondary Colors
- **Charcoal 700**: `#4a4a4a` (Secondary text - replaces `#4f4f4f`)
- **Charcoal 600**: `#5f5f5f` (Tertiary text - replaces `#6d6d6d`)

### Usage Guide

#### Text Colors
- **Headings**: `#252525` (Charcoal)
- **Body Text**: `#5f5f5f` (Charcoal 600)
- **Secondary Text**: `#4a4a4a` (Charcoal 700)

#### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Button**: `linear-gradient(135deg, #5A2633 0%, #4a1f2a 100%)`
- **Primary Button Hover Shadow**: `rgba(90, 38, 51, 0.3)`
- **Border Color**: `#5A2633`

#### Backgrounds
- **Primary Background**: `#F5F1E8`
- **Card Background**: `linear-gradient(135deg, #ffffff 0%, #fefdfb 100%)`
- **Section Gradient**: `linear-gradient(180deg, #F5F1E8 0%, #ffffff 50%, #F5F1E8 100%)`

#### Accents & Highlights
- **Gradient Text**: `linear-gradient(135deg, #5A2633 0%, #B49A67 100%)`
- **Icon Color**: `#5A2633`
- **Accent Glow**: `rgba(90, 38, 51, 0.15)`
- **Gold Accent**: `#B49A67`

#### Shadows & Effects
- **Elegant Shadow**: `0 4px 24px rgba(90, 38, 51, 0.08)`
- **Elegant Shadow Large**: `0 8px 40px rgba(90, 38, 51, 0.12)`
- **Premium Shadow**: `0 2px 8px rgba(90, 38, 51, 0.04), 0 8px 24px rgba(90, 38, 51, 0.06)`

## Design Philosophy

This color palette embodies:
- **Nordic Sophistication**: Refined burgundy inspired by Scandinavian design
- **Warm Professionalism**: Ivory background creates an approachable yet premium feel
- **International Appeal**: Colors that resonate across cultures and markets
- **Timeless Elegance**: A palette that won't feel dated in years to come

Perfect for a premium, international, world-class legal technology platform.
